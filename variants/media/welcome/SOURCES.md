## Central Asian footage (owner, 2026-11-08: «more Central Asian peoples»)

Searched Pexels, Pixabay and Mixkit in English, Russian and Uzbek and by city (Uzbekistan, Samarkand, Bukhara, Tashkent,
Kazakh/Kyrgyz family and couple, plov, dastarkhan, uzbek wedding, «узбекистан семья»). Found and used: the Uzbek wedding
courtyard (replaces the earlier family-table clip with East-Asian faces) and Bukhara. Not found under a free licence:
a Central Asian couple walking, or a Central Asian family at a dastarkhan with non, plov or tea, so the couple (from behind,
not identifiable) and the tea clip stay. Seen and rejected: Registan crowd (fine, but a crowd says the opposite of «not an
endless feed»), a grandmother with a baby at the Registan (the baby's face, a headscarf as the main motif), two elders in
doppi (doppi as the motif), a tour guide (identifiable faces), plov close-ups with a gloved hand, Mixkit clips under the
Restricted (personal-use) licence.
